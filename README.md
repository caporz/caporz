# Healthcare Management | Digital Transformation | Process Improvement

I work at the intersection of **healthcare management, data analysis, and digital transformation**.

My professional experience in healthcare administration and management has led me to focus on a practical question:

**How can technology and data help organizations improve the way they work?**

Rather than approaching digital transformation as a purely technological exercise, I believe in starting from operational needs, understanding existing processes, and developing solutions that generate measurable value.

## Areas of Interest

- **Healthcare Management:** organizational processes, administrative workflows, operational efficiency.
- **Process Improvement:** identifying inefficiencies and translating operational needs into practical solutions.
- **Data Analytics:** data processing, reporting, monitoring, and decision support.
- **Process Automation:** reducing repetitive activities through accessible technologies.
- **Artificial Intelligence:** exploring AI agents and intelligent automation for practical applications.

Data analysis and reporting projects developed with R, including healthcare consumption analysis, data quality checks, anomaly identification, and economic monitoring.

### Process Automation
Excel, VBA, and Power Query solutions designed to simplify administrative workflows, automate reporting activities, and support management control.

Experimental projects exploring AI agents, automated content workflows, model integration, and task orchestration.

## My Approach

I follow a problem-oriented approach:

1. Understand the organizational process.
2. Identify inefficiencies and improvement opportunities.
3. Analyze the available data.
4. Design and implement a practical solution.
5. Evaluate its impact and limitations.

I am particularly interested in solutions that are sustainable, understandable, and usable by the people who work with them every day.

## Current Focus

Exploring how data analytics, automation, and artificial intelligence can support healthcare organizations, improve administrative processes, and give professionals more time to focus on higher-value activities.
